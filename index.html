How to use
Simple guide for everyone
👋 Hello! This app helps track income and expenses for each property. You don't need to know anything about computers — just follow the steps below. If something goes wrong, nothing gets permanently broken — you can always delete an entry and start again.

📅 How to record a new guest booking

1
Click "+ Booking" at the top right of the screen

A form will pop up on screen. Don't worry — you only need to fill in a few boxes.

2
Choose the property

Use the dropdown to select which unit the guest is staying in (e.g. "Unit 1 — IT Park").

3
Enter the guest's name

Type the guest's full name (e.g. "Juan dela Cruz").

4
Enter check-in and check-out dates

Tap the date boxes and a calendar will appear. The number of nights will fill in automatically!

5
Enter the amount received from Airbnb

This is the total money paid — type it in the "Amount received" box (e.g. 8500).

6
Leave the checkbox ticked and click "Save booking"

The management fee (10%) and cleaning fee (₱850) will be added automatically as expenses — you don't need to do this separately!

🧾 How to record an expense (a bill or cost)

1
Click "+ Expense" at the top right

A form will appear. This is for recording any cost — electricity, internet, condo dues, repairs, etc.

2
Choose the property and enter the date

Select which property the expense is for, then pick the date it was paid.

3
Describe what it was for

Write something short and clear (e.g. "VECO electricity June 2026" or "Plumber — fixed leaking pipe").

4
Pick a category and enter the amount

Choose the best-matching category from the list (e.g. "Utilities" for electricity, "Maintenance" for repairs). Then type the amount paid.

5
Click "Save expense"

That's it! The expense is saved and will appear in the Expenses and Ledger screens.

🔍 How to check what's happening

1
Dashboard — the big picture

Click "Dashboard" in the left menu to see total income, expenses, and profit across all properties. The charts update automatically.

2
Look at one property at a time

Use the "View property" dropdown at the bottom of the left menu to switch to a single property view.

3
Bookings — see all guest history

Click "Bookings" to see every guest, their dates, and how much profit each booking made.

4
Ledger — see every transaction in order

The Ledger shows every income and expense in date order, with a running balance — like a bank statement for the properties.

❓ Common questions

I made a mistake — how do I fix it? ▾
Go to Bookings or Expenses, find the entry, and click the 🗑 bin icon on the right to delete it. Then add it again with the correct details. Nothing is permanent until you save it.
Do I need to add the management fee and cleaning separately? ▾
No! When you add a booking and leave the checkbox ticked, the app automatically adds both the management fee (10% of income) and the cleaning fee (₱850) as expenses. You don't need to do anything extra.
Can I use this on my phone? ▾
Yes! Just open the same link in your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari). Everything works the same way on a phone screen.
Will the other person see what I enter? ▾
Yes — because the app is connected to a shared database, anything you save will appear on everyone else's screen within a few seconds. You're all looking at the same data.
What are the expense categories for? ▾
Categories group your expenses so you can see at a glance where money is going — for example, how much goes on electricity vs repairs. Use "Management fee" for Diane's fee, "Utilities" for VECO and internet, "Condo dues" for association fees, "Maintenance" for repairs, and "Other" for anything that doesn't fit.
What if the app looks stuck or doesn't load? ▾
Try refreshing the page (pull down on mobile, or press F5 on a laptop). If it still doesn't work, check that you have internet connection. The app needs internet to save and load data.
